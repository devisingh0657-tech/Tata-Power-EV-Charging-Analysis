# Tata Power EV Charging Analysis

WS Cube Tech milestone project (Cohort 136) by Devi Singh.
Practice project on a Tata Power-style EV charging dataset. Not an official Tata Power work.

## Objective
Evaluate performance, profitability and expansion potential of an EV charging network across districts, using a 4-page Excel dashboard.

## Dataset
65 stations, 18 districts, 390 plugs, 114,000 charging sessions, 8,000 customers (Aug-Nov).

## Dashboard pages
1. Business and Usage Overview
2. Station Utilization and Operational Efficiency
3. Customer and Demand Behavior
4. Financial Performance and Expansion Strategy

## Key insights
- Sessions grew ~47% from Aug to Oct while the plug count stayed flat at 390.
- Demand peaks between 8 AM and 4 PM.
- Per-station load is even; district totals differ because stations per district range from 2 to 5.
- 5-year ROI ranges from about -22% to +145% by district, so expansion is prioritized in tiers.
